# RAG quality rubric (1–5)

Score the answer on a 5-point scale.

- **5** — answer is factually grounded in the contexts, directly addresses
  the question, and does not introduce material absent from the contexts.
- **4** — substantively correct and addresses the question, with minor
  wording or completeness issues.
- **3** — partially correct: addresses the question but omits a key fact
  the contexts make available, or includes a small unsupported claim.
- **2** — misses the main point of the question or contains material that
  contradicts the contexts.
- **1** — answer is not grounded in the contexts (hallucination), refuses
  when the contexts contain the answer, or is off-topic.

Penalize unsupported claims more heavily than minor wording issues.
